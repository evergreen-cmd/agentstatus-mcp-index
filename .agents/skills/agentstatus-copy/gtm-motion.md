# GTM Motion (Sep 14 to Oct 4) — copywriter context

Source: team GTM Motion doc. Use for cadence and conversion; do not invent a different goal.

## Goal
Self-serve signups. Soft ask. Not “book a meeting” as the default.

| Metric | 3-week total aim |
|--------|------------------|
| Signups | ~58 |
| Tagged social posts | ~90 |
| Videos | ~6 |
| Outreach touches | ~160 |

## Loop
1. Pick MCP from target list  
2. Run 18-host matrix + connection + tool history  
3. Select one infographic  
4. Post LinkedIn + X (tag company + responsible people)  
5. Touch DM/email: link + one thing to fix  
6. Log Notion; measure views → signups  

Every scorecard is three assets: **SEO page · social artifact · outreach first sentence.**

## Publishing lanes
- **Lane A:** notify first, 48h grace, then publish either way  
- **Lane B:** publish first, notify on publish  

If lane unspecified, write both notify variants or ask.

**Locked signup close (operator):**  
`If you want to see why [host] failed and what to change, feel free to create an account at agentstatus.dev.`

Do not close with “start free on…” or the fragment “Free account shows the client error and what to fix.”  
In outreach/social, say “across AI hosts” (and name apps). Do not say “host matrices.”

**Lane A must:** open with who is writing (`I'm [Name] on the AgentStatus team`); name `[Company]'s MCP` in the finding; use spoken counts (*eight of eighteen*); *but* before never-connected; separate “For [Host] specifically…” line; prefer *tool invoke / invocations* over dense `tools/call` packing in the notify.

## Infographic / comms policy
- Factual, reproducible, timestamped  
- No “broken”; full description e.g. failed a tool invoke in 18 of 18 hosts on 15 Sep 2026, 15:47 UTC  
- Default claim voice: *completed a tool invoke* / *tool invocations* with a subject. Do not default to `Cleared tools/call…`
- Keep evidence visible when possible (path, args, response)
