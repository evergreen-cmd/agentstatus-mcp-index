# Artificial Analysis craft notes

Evidence bank for `agentstatus-wording`. Steal structure; do not copy AA brand or index names onto AgentStatus surfaces.

## Homepage / chart chrome

- Tagline shape: *Independent analysis of AI*
- Job line: *Understand the AI landscape to choose the best model and provider for your use case*
- Axis formula: `Metric · Higher is better` / `Metric · Lower is better`
- Definition + direction in one line: *Weighted average cost (USD) per Intelligence Index task · Lower is better*
- Version notes are factual, not hype: *Intelligence Index v4.3 replaces X with Y, and upgrades Z to v4.0*

## Methodology voice

- Scope sentences open with what they **do**: *Artificial Analysis performs intelligence, quality, performance and price benchmarking…*
- Honest limit: results *are not intended to represent the maximum possible performance… they are intended to represent the real-world performance customers experience*
- Definitions list: *Model / Endpoint / Provider / Token* — noun, then one plain sentence
- Calculation sentences stay procedural: *This is calculated by… weighted by… then dividing by…*

## Article lede (Benchmarking GPT-6 Astra, 9 Sep 2026)

Pattern: **result → cost frame → then sections**

1. Tie/lead claim + cost advantage in sentence 1–2  
2. Pricing delta as hard numbers (`$4/$20` → `$10/$50`)  
3. Index-specific paragraphs restating score + % cost  
4. Bullet takeaways: bold claim fragment + evidence numbers  
5. Verbs: *ties, scores, gains, sits on, driven by, defines, leads, observes*

## X (@ArtificialAnlys)

Pattern from public posts:

- First line = who moved + score or frontier claim  
- Model names full and specific (effort levels when needed)  
- One link to analysis  
- Minimal flourish punctuation  
- Example shapes: *overtakes… with a score of…*; *the … frontier moved out substantially*; *You can test… on your own custom benchmarks*
